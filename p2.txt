STEP 2 — SOLAR REDUCTION
==================================================

For affected hour h:

effective_solar[h] =
original_solar[h] * factor

factor means FRACTION REMAINING.

Example:

solar = 180
factor = 0.25

effective solar =
45 kWh.

Outside the affected hours:

effective solar =
original solar.

If multiple valid solar reduction rules affect the same hour, handle composition consistently with the official semantics and do not silently discard constraints.

Because official scoring scenarios are stated to avoid mutually contradictory hard directives, prioritize mathematically valid composition.
